What is Montessori?

Montessori is an approach to early childhood education developed by Dr. Maria Montessori — built on the idea that children learn best when they're given freedom to explore within a carefully prepared environment. Rather than sitting and being taught, children choose their work, follow their curiosity, and build real skills at their own pace.

For many families in Midland, this may be your first encounter with Montessori — and that's exactly why we're here. We're introducing something different: an environment that respects children as capable, curious people.

🤲
Hands-On Learning Children work with real materials — not worksheets. They pour, sort, build, and discover.
🧭
Child-Led Each child chooses their work. Educators observe, guide, and introduce — never push.
🌱
Prepared Environment Every item in the room has a purpose. The space itself teaches order, focus, and care.
🕐
Unhurried Days Long, uninterrupted work periods let children develop deep concentration and independence.

Quick FAQ

Are you open now?

Yes — Tiffin Bay Montessori is now open and welcoming full-time enrollments. We are building our class and spaces are limited. Submit an inquiry above to get started.

What ages do you serve?

We serve children from 18 months through pre-kindergarten age. Contact us if you're unsure whether your child qualifies.

What is CWELCC and what does $22/day mean?

CWELCC is the Canada-Wide Early Learning and Child Care program. Through CWELCC, eligible families pay a reduced parent fee — currently targeted at $22/day at Tiffin Bay Montessori. We have 31 CWELCC-funded spaces. Contact us for current fee details.

What makes Montessori different from regular daycare?

Montessori is a full educational approach — not just childcare. Children work with hands-on materials, choose their own activities, and develop independence, concentration, and a love of learning. Our director is trained in Montessori and is thoughtfully introducing this approach to Midland families.
